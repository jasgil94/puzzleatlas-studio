SHIPPING FORECAST
Issued by the Met Office, 1755 UTC, Friday 21 November 1986
(clipped from the Shipping page, West Highland Free Press)

The following are warnings of gales in Sea Areas: Malin, Hebrides, Bailey, Fair Isle.

GENERAL SYNOPSIS AT 1200:
Low, 951, 150 miles west of Hebrides, expected 100 miles north of Hebrides by 0600 tomorrow, deepening further.

AREA FORECASTS FOR THE NEXT 24 HOURS:

HEBRIDES:
Southwesterly severe gale 9 to storm 10, veering northwesterly violent storm 11 for a time later. Very rough or high, occasionally phenomenal. Rain then squally showers. Poor, occasionally very poor.

MALIN:
Southwesterly storm 10, veering northwesterly severe gale 9 later. High. Rain at times. Poor.

BAILEY:
Southwesterly violent storm 11, veering northwesterly storm 10 later. Very high. Squally showers, thundery at times. Very poor.

Coastguard advise that conditions in these areas are among the most severe recorded so far this season, and small craft and inshore vessels are strongly advised against putting to sea.
