SHIPPING FORECAST
Issued by the Met Office, 1755 UTC, Friday 14 November 1986
(clipped from the Shipping page, West Highland Free Press)

The following are warnings of gales in Sea Areas: Malin, Hebrides, Bailey, Fair Isle.

GENERAL SYNOPSIS AT 1200:
Low, 954, 200 miles west of Rockall, expected 250 miles north-west of Rockall by 0600 tomorrow, deepening rapidly.

AREA FORECASTS FOR THE NEXT 24 HOURS:

HEBRIDES:
Southwesterly gale 8 to severe gale 9, increasing storm 10 for a time, veering northwesterly later. Rough or very rough, occasionally high. Rain then squally showers. Moderate, occasionally poor.

MALIN:
Southwesterly gale 8, increasing severe gale 9 to storm 10 later. Very rough or high. Rain at times. Moderate or poor.

BAILEY:
Southwesterly severe gale 9 to violent storm 11, veering northwesterly, decreasing gale 8 later. High or very high. Squally showers. Poor becoming moderate.
